Primary findings
- The earlier mixed-load intuition from Phase 010 was benchmark-shape-inflated by event-loop starvation and should not be reused as the steady-state throughput story.
- JSON marshalling is not a meaningful bottleneck at current scales (`~1-2 us/op`).
- Claim batching is the most promising near-term throughput lever discovered in this phase.
- The worker-side lifecycle path remains materially heavier than raw ingest.
- Multiple independent `knocker.open(...)` handles against one SQLite file are a degraded contention mode; corrected threaded probes surfaced sharp slowdown and real `database is locked` failures during worker claim (`BEGIN IMMEDIATE`).
